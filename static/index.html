Mix & master feedback for electronic music

Find out why your track doesn't hit like your references.

Upload your mix or master and one to three reference tracks. Fix the Mix measures kick, low end, tonal balance, loudness and stereo, compares matching sections like drop against drop, and writes a report that shows the evidence behind every suggestion.

WAV or MP3 Up to 30 MB per file 20 seconds to 10 minutes Your track + 1–3 references
  1. Add your track and at least one reference, with a note on what each reference is for.
  2. Say what you want from the comparison, or leave it blank for a general check.
  3. Check the sections it finds (intro, build, drop…) and fix any it got wrong.
  4. Read the report: findings, graphs, timestamps and what to try next.
Step 2

Check the sections

These sections are suggestions from the audio. Drag a boundary to move it, split or merge sections, and relabel anything that's wrong. Comparisons match drop to drop and build to build, so it pays to get these right. Your edits become the map the analysis uses.

Space play / pause S split at cursor M merge with next + − or pinch to zoom drag the waveform to scroll hold Alt while dragging a boundary to ignore the grid
Step 3

Sections confirmed
